1. What Rastro is

Rastro is an app for tracking habits, writing a journal and creating reminders, with a monthly review built from what you recorded. By using the app, you agree to these terms.

2. It is not a health service

  • Rastro is not therapy, diagnosis, treatment or monitoring of mental or physical health.
  • The review is a statistical summary of what you recorded: counts, averages and excerpts of your own writing. It does not interpret your emotional state and does not replace the help of a professional.
  • If you are going through a difficult time, please reach out to a health professional or a support service in your area. In Brazil, CVV answers at 188, at any hour.

3. Your data and your device

Your records stay on your device. Because of that, if you lose the device, uninstall the app or erase its data without having turned on iCloud sync, we cannot recover what you wrote, because we do not hold a copy. Keeping a backup is your responsibility. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

4. Reminders and alarms

Notifications depend on the device's operating system. Focus modes, battery saving, denied permissions or system failures can stop a notification from appearing. Do not use Rastro as the only way to remember something critical, such as a medication or an essential appointment.

5. Voice and transcription

Speech recognition is done by the device's operating system and can make mistakes. The punctuation added to the journal is automatic and approximate. Review the text before relying on it.
