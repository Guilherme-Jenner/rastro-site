rastro

The trail of what you've already done.

Rastro is an app for habits, journaling and reminders. You check off what you did, write or talk about your day, and at the end of the month you see, in numbers and in your own words, the path you've walked.

How it works

  • Habits: one tap a day, with a dot calendar that shows the month's consistency.
  • Journal: one page a day, with mood and text. You can write or dictate, and highlight the sentences you want to keep.
  • Voice reminders: say what you need to remember. Rastro understands the day and time and sets the alarm.
  • Monthly review: a story-style summary that brings together habits, mood and what you wrote.

Privacy, in one sentence

Your habits, journal and reminders stay only on your device, in an encrypted database. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. iCloud sync is optional.

What Rastro is not

  • It is not therapy, diagnosis or mental health monitoring.
  • It does not analyze your text on any server: the review is calculated on your device.
  • It does not promise to change your habits. It only records, honestly, what you did yourself.
