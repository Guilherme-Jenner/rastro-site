Frequently asked questions

My habit notifications don't show up.

In your device's Settings, check that Rastro notifications are allowed and that no focus mode is silencing them. On Android, also check battery saving. In the app, open the habit and see whether "Alarm on device" is on. If you marked the habit as done today and left "Skip if already marked" on, today's notification is skipped on purpose.

Voice dictation doesn't work.

Rastro transcribes your voice on the device itself, without sending it over the internet. For that, the language must be available offline. On iPhone, turn on dictation and add English to the languages in Settings › General › Keyboard. Also allow the microphone and speech recognition when the app asks.

How do I sync with another device?

Use the cloud button on the Home screen and turn on iCloud sync. Both devices must use the same Apple ID with iCloud enabled. Your data goes to your iCloud, encrypted.

How do I erase everything?

Uninstalling the app erases the data on the device. To also erase what is in iCloud: Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Rastro › Delete Data.

The Apple Watch doesn't show my habits.

Open Rastro on your iPhone once so it can send today's summary to the watch. The summary is valid for the current day: if you see "Open Rastro on your iPhone to update the day", just open the app on the iPhone. Both devices must be paired.

The monthly review didn't appear.

It is generated when you open the app on the last day of the month or, if you didn't open it that day, the next time you do. It is only created if there are habit check-ins or journal pages from that month.

I lost my data.

Since everything stays on your device, with no copy on a server, we can't recover what was erased unless iCloud sync was on. That's why we recommend turning it on.
