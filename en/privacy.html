Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11 · updated on 2026-11-08 · Português

In short: Rastro has no account, no server of its own and does not collect your data. Habits, journal and reminders stay only on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, they go to your iCloud, which we cannot access.

1. Who we are

Rastro is a habits, journal and reminders app for iPhone and Android, developed by Guilherme Jenner. To talk about personal data, use the contact in section 12.

2. What Rastro collects

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no login and no Rastro server that receives your information. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share data.

3. What stays on your device

Everything you create in the app is stored locally:

  • your habits, the days you checked them off and each habit's reminder settings;
  • journal pages (text, mood and input mode) and the excerpts you highlighted;
  • your reminders and the reviews that were generated;
  • your first-use answers and preferences, such as the theme and the daily reminder time.

This data lives in an encrypted database (AES-256). The key is kept in the system's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android) and never leaves the device.

4. Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone and speech recognition: used only when you tap to speak. Transcription happens on the device itself. Audio is not recorded or stored; the app keeps only the text you confirm. If the language is not available offline on the device, dictation does not work, instead of sending your voice to the cloud.
  • Notifications: alerts for your habits and reminders are scheduled on the device itself. No server of ours takes part.
  • Calendar (optional): if you choose "Add to calendar", Rastro creates, in the device's calendar, the events for the habits and reminders you asked for, and removes them when you turn the option off or delete the item. It does not read the rest of your schedule for other purposes. If your calendar is synced with an account (iCloud, Google), that account keeps the events, under its own rules.

You can deny or revoke any permission in the device's Settings. The app keeps working, without the corresponding feature.

5. iCloud sync (optional)

It is off until you turn it on. When on, Rastro stores your data in your private iCloud database (CloudKit), with end-to-end encryption, so your devices stay identical. We, as developers, have no access to this content. Apple uses its own notification service to tell your other devices that something changed. The storage counts against your iCloud quota and follows Apple's privacy policy.

6. Apple Watch and widgets

If you use an Apple Watch, the iPhone sends the paired watch today's summary (habit names, which are done and the next time), through Apple's system. Widgets also read, on the device itself, a summary of the day (habit progress and the next reminder). This content goes nowhere else.

7. Sharing

The review image is created on the device and handed to the system share sheet. You choose where it goes. It contains only the month, the opening line and the consistency rate; journal quotes and highlighted excerpts are not included. Rastro does not send the image anywhere.

8. Third parties

Rastro includes no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. The system services it uses (iCloud, notifications, calendar, speech recognition) have their own policies, from Apple and Google.

9. How to erase your data

  • On the device: uninstalling the app erases the database and the encryption key.
  • In iCloud: Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage › Rastro › Delete Data. This removes the data from iCloud, and the app turns sync off.
  • Calendar events: deleting the habit or turning off "Add to calendar" removes the event the app created.

Since we do not keep your data, there is nothing to erase on our side.

10. Your rights (LGPD and similar laws)

Brazil's data protection law (LGPD), like similar laws elsewhere, grants rights such as access, correction, deletion and portability. Because the data is with you, you exercise those rights in the app and on your device: everything Rastro keeps, you can see, edit and delete there. For any question or request, write to the contact below.

11. Children and teenagers

Rastro does not collect data from anyone, including children and teenagers: there is no account, sign-up, ads or sharing of information. Minors should use the app with a parent or guardian's knowledge, especially if they turn on iCloud sync or calendar access.

12. This site and contact

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies or analytics tools on it, but GitHub may log visits (such as the IP address) under its own policy. For questions, requests or any right above: guilhermejenner@clotheapp.com

13. Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page, with the update date.
